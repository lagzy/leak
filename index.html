how to play

real-life hide & seek around Root, with chaotic buffs.

1 · starteveryone meets at the start corner. pick hider / seeker / random.
2 · hidehiders get 10 minutes to hide inside the rectangle. seekers count down.
3 · seekseekers hunt. find a hider in real life, then tap catch on their name.
4 · casinoevery 12 minutes (host-set) a casino opens: spin, get 2 buff offers, keep one. seeker buffs sabotage hiders (scream orders, radar circles, ringing phones). hider buffs fight back (slow-mo, decoys, 20 m shifts).
5 · heartsbreak a rule (leave the zone, ignore a scream, get reported) → lose 1 of 3 ♥. zero hearts = you lose automatically.
6 · winseekers win by catching every hider before time runs out. hiders win by surviving.
